## Core argument
The spine of the book in 3 to 6 paragraphs. What thesis does the author
advance, how do they build it, and what do they conclude?

## Key themes
Four to eight themes, each a `### ` heading followed by a short paragraph.
Name the theme in the author's own vocabulary where they supply one.

## Chapter-by-chapter

following the order of the summaries below.

## People, places and dates
A bullet list, merged across sections and in chronological order where the
text gives dates, of the named people, places and dated events a reader of a
history timeline would want to look up. Keep the author's spellings.

## Takeaways
Six to twelve bullets. Each one a concrete, usable idea from the book, stated
so it stands on its own without the surrounding context.
